What it is, and isn't

Mock Anything simulates APIs on your computer for development and testing. Simulated responses are approximations built from public information and may differ from the real services. It does not process payments, send messages or emails, or connect to any provider unless you explicitly ask it to fetch a document. Before relying on an integration in production, test it against the provider's own sandbox.
